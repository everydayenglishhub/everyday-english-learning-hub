LISTENING CHALLENGE • BEGINNER

At morning tea

Two colleagues are talking during a break. What does the first person suggest?

Computer-generated English speech. A voice labelled en-NZ is preferred if your device provides one; this is not a recording of a Kiwi speaker.

Sam: “D'you wanna grab a cuppa?”

Alex: “Yeah, sounds good. Give us a sec.”

In clearer English: “Do you want to get a cup of tea or coffee?” / “Yes, that sounds good. Give me a second.”

Notice: “D'you” represents how “do you” can run together; “wanna” is an informal representation of “want to”; “cuppa” means a cup of tea or coffee. “Give us a sec” can mean “Give me a moment” in casual speech.

Sam: තේ හෝ කෝපි එකක් බොන්න යමුද?

Alex: හා, හොඳයි. මට පොඩි වෙලාවක් දෙන්න.
